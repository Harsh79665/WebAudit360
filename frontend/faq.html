Exhaustive methodology, scoring formulas, testing boundaries, and crawl safeguards

A single-page audit inspects only the exact URL submitted and its directly loaded subresources. A full-website audit begins at the seed URL, retrieves robots.txt and sitemap.xml, and traverses internal hyperlinks within configured depth and page limits.
When all categories are measured, the overall score uses the versioned algorithm: Overall = (0.25 × Performance) + (0.20 × SEO) + (0.20 × Accessibility) + (0.25 × Security) + (0.10 × Best Practices). If modules are omitted or unavailable, the weights are dynamically normalized only across measured modules, and omitted categories are explicitly marked as N/A.
Automated tools such as axe-core evaluate approximately 30% to 40% of WCAG success criteria (contrast, ARIA syntax, form labels). Criteria requiring cognitive context, meaningful text descriptions, and screen reader flow must be verified manually.
Active vulnerability testing with OWASP ZAP is prohibited against third-party sites without written consent. Target domain owners must complete a cryptographic DNS TXT challenge (_webaudit360-challenge.<domain>) or HTTP well-known token challenge before active tests can be enqueued.
Every submitted URL, discovered crawl link, and redirect target is resolved to its IP addresses and validated against RFC 1918 private subnets, loopback ranges (127.0.0.0/8, ::1), link-local addresses, and cloud provider metadata IPs (169.254.169.254). Outbound socket pinning prevents DNS rebinding.
